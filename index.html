Accountability Matrix

What We Measure

We reject vanity platform screenshots. Our performance is measured against real commercial viability.

Contribution Margin (CM)

Net revenue minus COGS, shipping, payment gateway fees, and ad spend. Our primary compass for true business viability.

Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)

Total top-line revenue divided by total marketing spend. Eliminates cross-channel attribution double-counting.

New Customer CAC (nCAC)

Total paid ad spend divided strictly by new, non-repeat buyers. Ensures acquisition isn't secretly relying on existing customers.

60-Day & 90-Day Cohort LTV

Cumulative gross margin generated by a customer cohort over time. Dictates how aggressively we can bid upfront.

Total ACOS / TACoS

Total ad spend on marketplaces divided by total marketplace revenue. The truest indicator of organic momentum.

Return-To-Origin (RTO) Rate

Percentage of shipped COD orders that fail delivery. Monitored closely to protect cash flow and fulfillment overhead.
