What We Measure

Commercial outcomes, not platform screenshots.

Contribution Margin

Revenue minus COGS, shipping, fees and ad spend.

MER

Total revenue divided by total marketing spend.

New Customer CAC

Ad spend divided by new buyers only.

Cohort LTV

Gross margin per customer cohort at 60 and 90 days.

TACoS

Marketplace ad spend divided by total marketplace revenue.

RTO Rate

COD orders that fail delivery.
