2.7 WHAT WE MEASURE (ACCOUNTABILITY MATRIX)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Section Header]
  - Tag: "Accountability Matrix"
  - Heading: "What We Measure"
  - Lead: "We reject vanity platform screenshots. Our performance is measured
           against real commercial viability."

[Metric 1: Contribution Margin (CM)]
  "Net revenue minus COGS, shipping, payment gateway fees, and ad spend.
   Our primary compass for true business viability."

[Metric 2: Marketing Efficiency Ratio (MER)]
  "Total top-line revenue divided by total marketing spend.
   Eliminates cross-channel attribution double-counting."

[Metric 3: New Customer CAC (nCAC)]
  "Total paid ad spend divided strictly by new, non-repeat buyers.
   Ensures acquisition isn't secretly relying on existing customers."

[Metric 4: 60-Day & 90-Day Cohort LTV]
  "Cumulative gross margin generated by a customer cohort over time.
   Dictates how aggressively we can bid upfront."

[Metric 5: Total ACOS / TACoS]
  "Total ad spend on marketplaces divided by total marketplace revenue.
   The truest indicator of organic momentum."

[Metric 6: Return-To-Origin (RTO) Rate]
  "Percentage of shipped COD orders that fail delivery.
   Monitored closely to protect cash flow and fulfillment overhead."

2.10 FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ ACCORDION)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[Section Header]
  - Tag: "Clarity & Terms"
  - Heading: "Frequently Asked Questions"
  - Lead: "Everything you need to know about our partnership model, economics,
           and engagement structure."

[Q1] How is Squired Marketing different from traditional marketing agencies?
[A1] Most traditional agencies charge a percentage of spend and pass your
     account to junior trainees who prioritize vanity metrics like clicks and
     platform-reported ROAS. At Squired, senior operators manage your brand
     directly. We focus strictly on Contribution Margin (cash left after ad spend,
     COGS, and shipping), blended MER, and creative velocity.

[Q2] What monthly ad spend is required to work with Squired?
[A2] We typically partner with brands spending at least ₹1,00,000 / month
     (or $3,000 / month internationally) across paid media, or brands with
     verified product-market fit preparing for rapid scale. This ensures
     sufficient data density for rapid creative testing and statistical significance.

[Q3] Do you manage both D2C (Shopify) and Marketplaces (Amazon/Flipkart)?
[A3] Yes. In fact, running D2C and marketplaces together is one of our greatest
     strategic advantages. We coordinate paid search and social campaigns to
     prevent cross-channel cannibalization, defend your brand terms, and maximize
     total blended profitability.

[Q4] How do your contracts and engagement terms work?
[A4] We begin with a focused 90-day initial engagement. This provides enough
     runway to conduct the audit, overhaul campaign architecture, deploy creative
     testing sprints, and demonstrate measurable impact on your unit economics.
     After 90 days, we operate on a flexible rolling 30-day notice period.

[Q5] Who produces the ad creatives and video assets?
[A5] We provide comprehensive creative direction, direct-response scripts,
     hook variations, and graphic design frameworks. For UGC and product filming,
     we either work with your in-house creator network or coordinate with vetted
     direct-response creators, handling all brief-writing and post-production editing.

[Q6] How quickly can we launch after signing?
[A6] Our diagnostic and onboarding sprint takes 7 business days. During this period,
     we audit your tracking (CAPI, server-side events), build out the initial
     30-day creative roadmaps, and stage new campaign structures for your review
     before going live.
