About Squired Marketing

A specialist growth consultancy built for modern commerce brands that value contribution margin over empty vanity metrics.

Origin & Mission

Why We Built Squired

Squired Marketing was founded to fix a fundamental flaw in the modern agency model: agencies that celebrate 5x ROAS in screenshots while the founder's bank balance quietly shrinks.

Most growth agencies operate on churn-and-burn. They assign junior account managers to manage templates, blast generic ad creatives, and take credit for organic sales when algorithms do the heavy lifting.

We built Squired as a high-conviction, senior-led growth partner. We treat every rupee or dollar of your ad budget as if it were drawn from our own operating accounts. We analyze unit economics, contribution margins, inventory turn rates, and post-purchase retention before recommending budget increases.

Headquartered in Kolkata, we partner with ambitious D2C brands, marketplace sellers, and consumer enterprises across India and global markets.

First Principles

What We Believe

Six non-negotiable operational principles that govern every campaign we deploy.

01

Cash in the Bank Beats In-Platform ROAS

Ad platform dashboards are designed to encourage more ad spend. We optimize for Contribution Margin and net bank deposits, never platform-reported vanity numbers.

02

Creative is the New Targeting

In the era of automated algorithmic delivery, ad creative does the targeting. A systematic, high-velocity creative production pipeline is your only durable moat.

03

First-Order Profit is Fragile; Retention is Compounding

Customer acquisition gets you in the game; retention is how you win. We build automated WhatsApp and email systems that transform one-off buyers into long-term gross margin.

04

No Junior Account Handoffs

You partner directly with senior operators who understand full-funnel economics, supply chains, and consumer psychology. No layer of intermediary account managers.

05

Attribution Must Be Blended and Honest

We don't argue over whether Google or Meta deserves credit for an order. We evaluate blended MER and new customer acquisition velocity as one unified commercial machine.

06

Ruthless Elimination of Waste

We cut non-performing search terms, fatigued ad creatives, and leaky landing pages without hesitation. Efficiency comes from stopping what does not work.
